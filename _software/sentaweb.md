SENTAWEB is an online tool for the automatic extraction of multiword expressions: sequences of lexical units (characters, words or punctuation marks), contiguous or not, that behave as syntactic-semantic units with their own meaning.

*Supported by national funding from FCT - Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia.*
