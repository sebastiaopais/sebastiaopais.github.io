Sebastião Pais is an **Assistant Professor** at the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.di.ubi.pt/) of the [University of Beira Interior](https://www.ubi.pt/) (UBI), Portugal, where he has taught and conducted research since 2016. He is a researcher at [NOVA LINCS](https://nova-lincs.di.fct.unl.pt/) (Portugal) and collaborates with the [CNRS GREYC UMR 6072 laboratory](https://www.greyc.fr/) (France).

His research lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning and Affective Computing, with a particular interest in methods that are reliable, explainable and socially useful.

## Research interests

- **Affective computing and multimodal sentiment analysis**: modelling emotion and opinion across text, audio and video.
- **Computational mental health**: early detection of depression and other mental-health signals from language and social media.
- **Trustworthy large language models**: explainability and machine unlearning in LLMs.
- **Information retrieval, text mining and social network analysis.**

## Background

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science in 2013 from MINES ParisTech (now [Mines Paris – PSL](https://www.minesparis.psl.eu/), France) and the University of Beira Interior, with the thesis [*Asymmetric Distributional Similarity Measures to Recognize Textual Entailment by Generality*](https://pastel.hal.science/pastel-00962176). In 2017 he was granted the French national qualification for the position of Maître de Conférences (MCF) in Computer Science.

He is a Professional Member of the [ACM](https://www.acm.org/) and a member of the [French Association for Artificial Intelligence (AFIA)](https://afia.asso.fr/).

## Prospective students
